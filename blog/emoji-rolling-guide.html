《Emoji Rolling》是一款基于真实物理引擎的爬坡游戏。乐趣不在于"成功登顶"——翻车本身往往比成功更搞笑。但如果你真想征服那些疯狂的地形，你需要理解物理。

🌀 A broken tractor, impossible terrain, and you — who flips over a lot.

Emoji Rolling is a physics-based hill climbing game. The fun isn't in "reaching the top" — the flip itself is often funnier than success. But if you really want to conquer those crazy hills, you need to understand physics.

🎮 核心机制：油门、重心、摩擦力 🎮 Core Mechanics: Gas, Center of Gravity, Friction

  • 油门控制：按住屏幕右侧加速，左侧刹车/倒车。油门不是"越大越好"——爬坡时过大的油门会让前轮离地，直接翻车。
  • 重心动态：车辆重心随地形实时变化。上坡时重心后移，需要更多油门；下坡时重心前移，需要刹车减速。
  • 摩擦力：不同地形摩擦系数不同。草地>土路>冰面。冰面上油门大了会原地打滑，小了爬不动。
  • Gas control: Hold right side to accelerate, left side to brake/reverse. More gas isn't always better — too much on a climb lifts the front wheels and flips you.
  • Dynamic COG: Vehicle center of gravity shifts with terrain. Uphill = COG moves back, needs more gas. Downhill = COG moves forward, needs braking.
  • Friction: Different terrain has different friction. Grass > dirt > ice. On ice, too much gas = wheelspin; too little = no climb.

核心真相：翻车的90%原因不是"地形太难"，而是"油门太猛"。物理游戏里最危险的永远是你自己的右脚。

Core truth: 90% of flips aren't because "the terrain is too hard" — they're because "the gas is too heavy." In physics games, the most dangerous thing is always your own right foot.

🎯 爬坡核心技巧：三段式油门法 🎯 Core Climbing Technique: Three-Stage Gas

阶段一：蓄力期（坡底）

Stage 1: Charge (Bottom of Hill)

  • 倒车拉距离：如果坡很陡，先倒车5-10米，留出"助跑"空间。
  • 匀速接近：接近坡道时保持中等速度，不要在坡底突然加速——重心前移会导致前轮离地。
  • Reverse for distance: If the slope is steep, reverse 5-10m first to create a "runway."
  • Approach steadily: Maintain medium speed near the base. Don't suddenly accelerate at the bottom — COG shifts forward, lifting front wheels.

阶段二：冲刺期（坡中）

Stage 2: Sprint (Mid-Slope)

  • 最大油门：这是唯一可以"地板油"的阶段。重心在后，后轮抓地力最大。
  • 观察前轮：如果前轮开始离地（车头抬起），立刻松油门0.5秒让车头回落，再继续。
  • Max gas: This is the only stage where "floor it" is allowed. COG is back, rear wheels have max traction.
  • Watch front wheels: If they start lifting (nose rising), release gas for 0.5s to let the nose drop, then resume.

阶段三：收油期（坡顶）

Stage 3: Ease (Top of Hill)

  • 提前收油：看到坡顶时，提前1秒松油门。登顶后立刻面对的是下坡或平地，高速冲出=翻车。
  • 准备刹车：手指移到左侧刹车区，随时准备。很多翻车发生在"以为终于登顶了"的那一刻。
  • Release early: When you see the top, release gas 1 second before reaching it. After summiting, you face a descent or flat — high speed exiting = flip.
  • Brake ready: Move finger to left brake zone, always prepared. Many flips happen at the "I finally made it!" moment.
关键口诀："坡底稳，坡中冲，坡顶收。" 违反任何一条=翻车。 Key mantra: "Steady at the bottom, punch in the middle, ease at the top." Violate any = flip.

💡 翻车后的正确态度 💡 The Right Attitude After Flipping

  • 翻车是游戏的一部分：物理引擎的不可预测性正是乐趣所在。别懊恼，笑一笑，重来。
  • 复盘比重试重要：每次翻车后问自己："是油门大了？还是重心没控制好？"找到原因才能进步。
  • 升级优先级：引擎>轮胎>车身。引擎提供动力，轮胎提供抓地力，车身减少翻车概率。
  • Flipping is part of the game: The unpredictability of physics is the fun. Don't rage — laugh, restart.
  • Review matters more than retry: After each flip, ask: "Too much gas? Or bad COG control?" Finding the cause is progress.
  • Upgrade priority: Engine > Tires > Body. Engine = power. Tires = traction. Body = flip resistance.

🏆 极限地形通关秘诀 🏆 Extreme Terrain Clear Tips

  • "之字形"爬坡：面对超过45度的陡坡，不要直冲。斜着上（左-右-左），每次只爬一小段，利用横向摩擦力辅助。
  • "借力打力"：利用地形凸起做"弹跳"——在凸起处快速点按油门，让车辆"弹"起来越过障碍。
  • "倒车复位"：如果车头翘起太高（即将后翻），立刻切换到倒车+刹车，让车头回落。这是救车的最后机会。
  • "Zigzag" climbing: Facing a >45° slope, don't charge straight. Go diagonally (left-right-left), each segment small, using lateral friction to assist.
  • "Borrow the terrain": Use terrain bumps as "launchers" — tap gas quickly at a bump to "bounce" over obstacles.
  • "Reverse recovery": If nose rises too high (about to backflip), immediately switch to reverse + brake to bring the nose down. Last chance save.
