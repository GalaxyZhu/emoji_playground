🗿 Emojis have invaded your screen. Your mission: blast them all.

Emoji Shooter is a vertical arcade shooter with one control: move. Your ship fires automatically. Don't let the simplicity fool you — surviving the dense bullet patterns and quirky bosses takes more than luck.

🎮 基础操作与生存法则 🎮 Basics & Survival Rules

  • 移动即射击：手指/鼠标控制飞机位置，自动射击。你唯一需要专注的是走位。
  • 预判弹幕：敌人的子弹有固定轨迹，观察1秒就能找到安全通道。不要急着冲，先看清楚。
  • 边角生存：屏幕两侧通常是弹幕密度最低的区域，被围时往边角靠。
  • Move = Shoot: Drag or mouse to position. Your ship fires automatically. Focus purely on positioning.
  • Read bullet patterns: Enemy shots follow fixed paths. Watch for 1 second to find the safe lane. Don't rush in blindly.
  • Edges are safe: Screen sides usually have the lowest bullet density. When surrounded, head for the corners.

👾 Boss战打法解析 👾 Boss Fight Breakdown

第一关Boss：😈 恶魔 Emoji

Stage 1 Boss: 😈 Devil Emoji

  • 攻击模式：散射弹幕 + 追踪火球
  • 弱点：追踪火球转弯慢，利用急停反向走位躲开
  • 打法：保持中距离，别贴脸。散射弹幕在近身时密度太高，中距离有足够的缝隙穿过
  • Attack pattern: Spread barrage + homing fireballs
  • Weakness: Homing fireballs turn slowly. Use sudden stops and reverse direction.
  • Tactic: Keep mid-range, don't go close. Spread barrage is too dense up close; mid-range has safe gaps.

第二关Boss：🤢 呕吐绿球

Stage 2 Boss: 🤢 Puke Green Ball

  • 攻击模式：全屏毒液喷射，地面残留毒池
  • 弱点：喷射前有明显蓄力动作（身体膨胀），看到蓄力立刻往两侧闪
  • 打法：毒池是持续伤害，宁可多走两步绕过去，也别硬穿。护盾留着挡毒池比挡弹幕更划算
  • Attack pattern: Full-screen poison spray, leaves toxic puddles
  • Weakness: Obvious charge-up (body swells) before spray. Dodge sideways when you see it.
  • Tactic: Puddles deal sustained damage. Walk around them, don't cut through. Save shield for puddles rather than bullets.

第三关Boss：🤡 小丑炸弹人

Stage 3 Boss: 🤡 Clown Bomber

  • 攻击模式：随机投放延时炸弹 + 快速冲刺
  • 弱点：炸弹落地后有3秒延时，足够你调整位置。真正危险的是冲刺——听到"嘀嘀"声立刻远离中心
  • 打法：这是第一个考验你"听声辨位"的Boss。关掉音乐开声音，提示音比视觉更早预警
  • Attack pattern: Random delayed bombs + fast dash charges
  • Weakness: Bombs have a 3-second delay after landing — enough time to reposition. The real danger is the dash. When you hear "beep beep," get away from center.
  • Tactic: This boss tests your audio awareness. Turn off music, keep sound on. Audio cues warn before visuals.

🎯 Boss通用法则：每个Boss出场前都有3秒无敌动画，利用这段时间调整位置到屏幕正下方——那里是最安全的起始点。

🎯 Universal Boss Rule: Every boss has a 3-second invincible intro. Use that time to position yourself at the bottom-center — the safest starting point.

🏆 高分玩家的秘密习惯 🏆 High-Score Player Habits

  • 不贪心：看到道具别急着冲过去捡，先确认周围弹幕轨迹。死在路上不如不捡。
  • 留退路：永远保证自己左右各有一条逃生路线。别把自己逼到墙角。
  • 节奏感：Boss战是有节奏的——攻击→蓄力→爆发→空窗期。在空窗期输出，其他时候专心躲。
  • 熟悉先于反应：第一次打新Boss肯定会死，那是交学费。死3次后你自然会知道哪里该往哪躲。
  • Don't be greedy: Don't rush to grab power-ups. Check bullet trajectories first. Dying on the way there isn't worth it.
  • Always have an exit: Keep an escape route on both sides. Never trap yourself in a corner.
  • Learn the rhythm: Boss fights have a beat — attack → charge → burst → window. DPS during the window; dodge everything else.
  • Familiarity beats reflexes: You'll die to a new boss. That's tuition. After 3 deaths, you'll instinctively know where to dodge.
