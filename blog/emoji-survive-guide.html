🎮 开局即决战：前3波的资源积累 🎮 The First 3 Waves: Your Investment Phase

很多人前3波就觉得"随便打"，结果第5波开始发现自己弹药不够、升级路线选错了。

Many players breeze through waves 1–3, only to find themselves out of ammo and stuck with bad upgrades by wave 5.

核心原则：前3波是投资期，不是挥霍期。

Core principle: Waves 1–3 are an investment phase, not a spending spree.

🧟‍♂️ 怪物类型与应对策略 🧟‍♂️ Enemy Types & Counter Strategies

废土上的怪物不是只会往前冲的傻子。它们有分工：

The wasteland's monsters aren't mindless — they have roles:

  • 🚶 普通丧尸 — 慢、血厚、数量多。用射速碾压，别浪费子弹在单发高伤上。
  • ⚡ 速度型 — 快得像打了鸡血。预判走位，提前开枪，等它到你脸前再射击已经晚了。
  • 🐘 坦克型 — 血厚到离谱。集中火力，别分散攻击。有燃烧伤害优先上。
  • 🏹 远程型 — 最阴险的。躲在后排放冷箭，优先击杀，不然你打着打着血莫名其妙就空了。
  • 🚶 Regular Zombies — Slow, tanky, numerous. Overwhelm with fire rate; don't waste high-damage single shots.
  • ⚡ Speed Type — Fast as adrenaline. Predict movement and pre-fire; shooting after they're in your face is too late.
  • 🐘 Tank Type — Absurdly tanky. Focus fire, don't spread damage. Burn damage is your friend here.
  • 🏹 Ranged Type — The sneakiest. Sniping from the back row. Kill them first, or your HP will vanish mysteriously.

击杀优先级：远程型 > 速度型 > 坦克型 > 普通丧尸

Kill priority: Ranged > Speed > Tank > Regular

记住这个顺序，你的命会值钱很多。🔥

Remember this order. Your life becomes much more valuable. 🔥

🏆 第15波之后的生存哲学 🏆 Wave 15+ Survival Philosophy

能活到这里的都是狠人。但第15波之后，游戏性质变了——从"打怪升级"变成"资源管理"。

Anyone still standing here is a badass. But after wave 15, the game changes — from "kill and level up" to "resource management."

  • 🔫 子弹管理：别看见怪就开枪，等它们扎堆再出手。一发子弹打三个，比三发子弹打一个省得多。
  • 🏃 走位原则：永远在移动。站着打靶等于找死。画圈走位，让怪物追你，而不是你追怪物。
  • 🔄 技能刷新：如果当前三个技能选项都很烂，宁愿跳过这轮。别为了升级而升级，一个垃圾技能会拖累你后面5波。
  • 🔫 Ammo management: Don't shoot at the first thing you see. Wait for clusters. One bullet hitting three enemies saves more than three bullets on one.
  • 🏃 Movement rule: Always be moving. Standing still is asking to die. Circle-strafe, make them chase you.
  • 🔄 Skill reroll: If all three skill choices suck, skip the round. A bad skill drags you down for the next 5 waves.
终极真相：第20波的Boss不是打死的，是磨死的。保持移动，别贪刀，活着才有输出。 The ultimate truth: The wave 20 boss isn't killed — it's outlasted. Keep moving, don't get greedy, staying alive IS your DPS.

🎖️ 高分玩家的小秘密 🎖️ High-Score Player Secrets

  • 每次升级弹匣容量后，记得补满子弹再进下一波。
  • 如果你的分数卡在15波过不去，回去重打第1-10波，专练走位。走位好了，后面轻松一半。
  • BOSS战时，绕圈跑比直线跑生存率高 3 倍。直线的尽头是墙角，绕圈没有尽头。
  • 燃烧伤害对Boss有效，但别指望靠燃烧烧死它——它只是帮你"磨血"的工具。
  • After every magazine upgrade, reload to full before the next wave.
  • Stuck at wave 15? Go back to waves 1–10 and practice movement. Good positioning halves the difficulty of later waves.
  • During boss fights, circling is 3x more survivable than running straight. Straight lines end in walls. Circles don't end.
  • Burn damage works on the boss, but don't expect to burn it to death — it's just a "grind helper."
