Emoji Arcade 用户隐私与虚拟资产保护说明 Emoji Arcade User Privacy & Virtual Asset Protection

一、我们收集的信息 1. Information We Collect

1.1 您主动提供的信息（用户账号） 1.1 Information You Provide (User Account)

随着用户系统的上线，我们通过 Firebase Authentication 收集以下信息：

With the launch of our User System, we collect information through Firebase Authentication:

  • 身份数据：您的唯一用户 ID (UID)、显示名称。
  • Identity Data: Your unique User ID (UID), display name.

1.2 游戏与虚拟资产数据 1.2 Game & Virtual Asset Data

为了提供持久的游戏体验，我们在 Google Firebase Firestore 中存储：

To provide a persistent gaming experience, we store the following in Google Firebase Firestore:

  • 游戏币 (🪙)：您的当前余额、每日签到时间戳及交易记录。
  • Arcade Coins (🪙): Your current balance, daily check-in timestamps, and transaction history.
  • 游戏进度：最高分记录及全球排行榜排名。
  • Game Progress: High scores and leaderboard rankings.

1.3 自动收集的信息 1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • 使用数据：游戏时长、访问页面及通过 Vercel Analytics 收集的技术日志。
  • Usage Data: Time spent on games, page visits, and technical logs via Vercel Analytics.
  • Cookies：用于维持您的登录会话并记住网站偏好设置。
  • Cookies: Used to maintain your login session and remember site preferences.

二、我们如何使用信息 2. How We Use Information

  • 身份验证：管理您的账号并确保初始金币在不同设备间同步。
  • Authentication: To manage your account and sync your initial balance across devices.
  • 每日奖励：验证签到时间戳以确认每日签到奖励资格。
  • Daily Rewards: To verify your check-in timestamp for daily bonus eligibility.
  • 商业化变现：通过 Google AdSense 投放激励视频广告，允许您通过观看广告赚取金币。
  • Monetization: To deliver Rewarded Ads via Google AdSense, allowing you to earn coins by watching videos.
  • 全球排行榜：在全球竞赛中展示您的昵称和分数。
  • Leaderboards: To display your nickname and score in global competitions.

三、虚拟资产免责声明（Arcade Coins） 3. Virtual Assets Disclaimer (Arcade Coins)

  • 无实际货币价值：Arcade Coins (🪙) 仅作为平台内模拟"投币"机制的虚拟积分。
  • Non-Monetary Value: Arcade Coins (🪙) are strictly virtual points used for in-game "coin-op" mechanics.
  • 不可退款/兑换：金币不具有任何现实世界的货币价值，不可提现、出售或兑换为法定货币。
  • No Refund/Exchange: Coins have no real-world monetary value and cannot be withdrawn, sold, or exchanged for real currency.
  • 资产管理：我们保留管理和调整虚拟资产余额的权利，以维护平台的公平性与完整性。
  • Management: We reserve the right to regulate and adjust virtual asset balances to maintain platform integrity.

四、第三方服务与 Cookies 4. Third-Party Services & Cookies

我们与以下必要服务商共享数据：

We share data with the following essential providers:

  • Google AdSense：使用 Cookies 根据您过往的访问记录投放广告。您可以通过访问 广告设置 选择停用个性化广告。
  • Google AdSense: Uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.
  • Google Firebase：我们主要的后端服务商，用于用户身份验证及安全的云端数据库管理 (Firestore)。
  • Google Firebase: Our primary backend for user authentication and secure database management (Firestore).
  • Vercel：我们的托管服务商及网站匿名分析工具。
  • Vercel: Our hosting and web analytics provider.

五、数据安全与您的权利 5. Data Security & Your Rights

  • 安全保障：所有数据均通过 HTTPS 加密传输，并受 Firebase 安全规则保护，防止未经授权的访问。
  • Security: All data is transmitted via HTTPS and secured by Firebase Security Rules to prevent unauthorized access.
  • 删除权：您拥有"被遗忘权"。您可以通过联系我们请求删除您的账号及所有关联的游戏数据。
  • Deletion: You have the "Right to be Forgotten." You may request the deletion of your account and all associated game data by contacting us.
  • 合规性：我们尊重全球隐私标准（如 GDPR/CCPA），并提供数据访问与更正工具。
  • GDPR/CCPA Compliance: We respect global privacy standards and provide tools for data access and rectification.

六、儿童隐私 6. Children's Privacy

Emoji Arcade 的游戏内容适合全年龄段，但根据 AdSense 政策要求，本网站不旨在收集 13 岁以下儿童的数据。

Emoji Arcade is a family-friendly platform, but in compliance with AdSense policies, we do not intentionally collect data from children under 13.
