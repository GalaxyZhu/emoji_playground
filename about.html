我们的故事

Our Story

你有没有刷短视频时，被那些"挂羊头卖狗肉"的小游戏广告骗过？画面里明明是个精致的益智游戏，点进去却是个粗糙的换皮产品，满屏弹窗逼你充钱，三天不玩账号就消失。

Have you ever been tricked by those misleading mobile game ads while scrolling through short videos? The ad shows a polished puzzle game, but after you click, it's a cheap reskin packed with pop-ups forcing you to pay — and your progress vanishes if you don't play for three days.

2026年初，我们想：为什么不能自己做出真正的好游戏？不用下载 App，不用注册账号，打开浏览器就能玩。而且，要有更多合家欢游戏——大人可以跟孩子一起玩，朋友可以一起对战，而不是一个人对着屏幕默默充值。

In early 2026, we asked ourselves: Why can't we make real games? No app downloads, no registration — just open the browser and play. And we wanted more family-friendly games — where parents play with kids, friends battle each other, instead of someone staring at a screen alone, endlessly paying.

Emoji Arcade 就这么诞生了。我们坚持用纯粹的 Web 技术——HTML5 Canvas + JavaScript，每一款游戏都是原创玩法，每一个 Emoji 都是亲手挑选。不依赖游戏引擎，不外包美术，确保打开网页的那一秒，游戏就已经准备好。

That's how Emoji Arcade was born. We stick to pure web tech — HTML5 Canvas + JavaScript. Every game has original gameplay, every Emoji is hand-picked. No game engines, no outsourced art. By the time the webpage opens, the game is already ready.

🎯 我们的使命

🎯 Our Mission

让游戏回归纯粹的快乐。无论是通勤路上打发时间，还是周末陪孩子比赛高分，甚至是深夜失眠想找点轻松的——打开浏览器，点开就能玩。我们相信，最好的游戏不需要说明书，最好的快乐不需要充值。

Bring the joy back to gaming. Commuting and need a quick distraction? Playing with your kid for the weekend high score? Can't sleep at 2am and need something light? Open your browser, click, and play. We believe the best games need no tutorial, and the best fun needs no payment.

Game Lineup

目前 Emoji Arcade 已上线 7 款精心打磨的休闲游戏：

Currently, Emoji Arcade features 7 carefully crafted casual games:

  • 🗿 表情大乱射 - 街机射击，消灭搞怪 Emoji 敌人
  • 🛻 废土求生 - 末日生存，改装皮卡对抗 20 波怪物
  • 🧩 记忆翻翻天 - 经典翻牌，支持双人对战
  • 🔗 Emoji连连看 - 经典连线消除，考验观察力与手速
  • 🏎️ 油门焊死 - 极简竞速，单手操控极速狂飙
  • 🚗 漂移狂魔 - 三车道跑酷，考验反应极限
  • 🏎️ 道具互坑赛 - 道具赛车，欢乐互坑
  • 🚜 Emoji Rolling - 从高山翻滚而下，飞跃裂缝
  • 🐸 跳跳蛙大冒险 - 按住跳，松开落，手速决定高度
  • 🐵 猴子过河 - 穿越车流、跳过河流、躲避鳄鱼

  • 🛻 Emoji Survive - Apocalypse survival, mod your truck against 20 waves
  • 🧩 Emoji Match - Classic memory game, supports 2-player battles
  • 🔗 Emoji Link-Up - Classic link-up puzzle, test your eyes & speed
  • 🏎️ Emoji Rush - Minimalist racing, one-hand speed control
  • 🚗 Emoji Drift - Three-lane parkour, test your reflexes
  • 🏎️ Emoji Kart - Item racing, fun chaos
  • 🚜 Emoji Rolling - Roll down mountains, jump over gaps
  • 🐸 Emoji Frog - Jump and dash, speed challenge
  • 🐵 Monkey Crossing - Cross roads, jump rivers, dodge crocs
